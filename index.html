ABOUT THE AUDIO

These examples use actual MPDD and Bazinga dialogue turns, preserving their text, speaker assignments, and emotion labels. The 14 excerpts cover seven emotions in both Mandarin and English. Two complete dataset dialogues include all six Mandarin turns and all five English turns, respectively.

MPDD emotion labels come from its original gold annotations. Bazinga labels come from Voxtral text-only predictions and are not gold annotations. The original speaker prompts are available as reference recordings.

IndexTTS-2 synthesis uses strength 0.6 for every emotion, including neutral, with emo_alpha = 1.0, seed 42, and random sampling disabled. The full-dialogue recordings concatenate generated turns in dataset order with 0.35-second gaps; they do not preserve original recording timing. Emotion labels describe the synthesis condition, not a perceptual evaluation.
